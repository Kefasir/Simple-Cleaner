Cleaner
A simple and lightweight PySide6 utility designed to clean system junk, temporary files, and cache. It helps you quickly free up space on your C: drive.
🚀 Features
The application offers three cleaning modes, switchable via the buttons at the bottom of the interface:
• Fast — quick cleanup of general temporary folders (Temp) and the Recycle Bin.
• Deep — deep cleaning, including browser cache (Chrome, Discord), Windows Explorer cache, shaders, error dumps, and the Downloads folder.
• Custom — flexible setup that lets you manually check or uncheck specific areas you want to clean.
🛠️ How It Works
1. Select Mode: Click one of the three buttons at the bottom (Fast, Deep, or Custom) and check the desired options in the pop-up settings window.
2. Scan: Click the SCAN button to estimate the total size of junk files. The progress bar will show the scanning status.
3. Clean: Click the CLEAN button to safely remove the selected data. The drive space indicator will automatically update in real time.
